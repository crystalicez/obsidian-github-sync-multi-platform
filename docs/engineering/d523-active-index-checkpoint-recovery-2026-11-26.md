Manual active-route Sync hashes all included physical bytes instead of trusting size/mtime, even on recovery retry. Startup/scheduled/local-change Sync and Force cannot initiate repair. Existing command notice guides missing-only repair versus Manual Recovery.

Limitations: a lost/corrupt checkpoint, changed HEAD/WAL, divergent existing artifact, initial activation before a successful Sync, or damaged historical route remain blocked. Existing corrupt artifacts are not overwritten. This is a cooperative best-effort filesystem protocol (exists/write is not an OS-wide exclusive-create guarantee), not proof against non-cooperating processes or power loss. Extra local disk space for one sharded index copy, normal manual active-route full-scan cost, additional read-only HEAD/WAL verification. No physical device qualification; no public Execute or Force authorization.
