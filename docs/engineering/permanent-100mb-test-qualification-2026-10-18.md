# Permanent 100 MB test policy and initial Windows qualification (2026-10-18)

## Maintainer decision

From 2026-10-18, test workloads use **no more than 100,000,000 decimal bytes**. Removed live/default 2 GiB and 5 GiB soak configurations and renamed the soak test to `v4-100mb-stream.test.ts`. Planner, feasibility, chunk upload/download and writer-memory fixtures now use at most 100 MB instead of old large sizes. `pnpm test:soak` defaults to 100 MB, and `--bytes=100000001` or `--bytes=5368709120` fail before any test starts. Direct `V4_SOAK_BYTES` is also bounded. `scripts/model-github-v4-large-file.mjs` defaults to 100 MB and refuses larger test inputs.

This policy does **not** change production V4 file-size/protocol limits. Historical evidence in earlier commits or old baseline sections is not an executable instruction.
